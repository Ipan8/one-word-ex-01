Entry for the make.fun bounty **"One Word From Your Ex"** — one word, the word
your ex would use to describe you, and nothing else.

## The entry

**Regret**

The funniest, most painfully honest word an ex could pick. It is the laugh
and the sting in a single hit: of course that is the word — and it probably
is true. "Ghosted" or "Avoid" describe what happened; "Regret" is the
verdict, the word an ex reaches for when asked to sum you up in one hit.
One word. No explanation. No appeal.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry word to `/output/entry.txt`:

```bash
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
# Regret
```
